RULES & REGULATIONS

  • Corporate Catalyst is a case-study-based competition where student teams analyse real-world corporate cases drawn from different business domains — Human Resources, Marketing, Finance, Operations & Supply Chain, and Business Strategy — and present innovative, practical solutions before a faculty jury.
  • Each team is assigned a case prepared by the organising team and works through problem identification, root cause analysis, solution design, implementation planning and expected outcomes.
  • The competition is designed to test strategic thinking, creativity, problem-solving, business understanding, communication, teamwork and decision-making.
  • The competition culminates in a live presentation and jury Q&A, where teams must clearly communicate and defend their proposed solutions.
  • Teams must follow the case instructions and timelines communicated by the organising committee.
  • Participants must report to the Seminar Hall before the scheduled reporting time.
  • All participants must carry a valid college or institution ID.
  • Any form of cheating, plagiarism, unauthorised assistance, misconduct or unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • The organising committee reserves the right to make final decisions on disputes, scoring and rule interpretation.
  • Participants must follow all venue, campus and event-day instructions provided by the organising committee.
