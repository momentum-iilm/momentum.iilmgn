RULES & REGULATIONS

The Inter-University Business Quiz is a competitive quiz designed for university management students, with a focus on business, economics, brands and current affairs.

  • The quiz gives students an opportunity to demonstrate their knowledge and quick thinking.
  • Participants will sharpen their ability to deduce answers from clues and appreciate the value of teamwork.
  • The question sets will be fully customised for the event, with no reused or generic content.
  • The quiz will be conducted by a professional quizmaster with national-level hosting experience.
  • Teams must consist of 3 students.
  • Participants must report to the Auditorium before the scheduled reporting time.
  • All participants must carry a valid college or institution ID.
  • Any form of cheating, misconduct, unauthorised assistance or unfair practice may result in disqualification.
  • The organising committee reserves the right to make final decisions on disputes, scoring and rule interpretation.
  • Participants must follow all venue, campus and event-day instructions provided by the organising committee.
